# Human operator

Derived from MakeHuman's hm08 base mesh, clothing helper surface, default skeleton and skin weights. These graphical assets were released under CC0.

- Source: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/tree/master/makehuman/data
- License: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.md
- Original mesh and skeleton: © 2020 Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier.
- Original skin weights: © 2021 Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier.

Adaptations: extracted clothed surface, face and hands; rescaled to 1.75 metres; retained four normalized skin influences per vertex; rendered as a cyan hologram with a procedural headset. Human movement is an illustrative reconstruction of robot motion, not recorded human tracking.
