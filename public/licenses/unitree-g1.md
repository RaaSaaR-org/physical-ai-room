# Unitree G1 EDU / Dex3 asset attribution

Copyright (c) 2016-2024 HangZhou YuShu TECHNOLOGY CO.,LTD. (Unitree Robotics).

The G1 EDU URDF and STL visuals were sourced from the local EmAI
`robot-management-system/app/public/assets/robots/g1` asset collection.
The local project identifies Unitree robot geometry as BSD 3-Clause licensed,
with upstream attribution to https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_mujoco.

For this learning room, the `g1_edu.urdf` visual geometry was simplified and
converted to `g1-dex3.glb`. All 43 actuated joints, including Dex3 hands, remain
in the scene hierarchy. `g1-rig.json` retains the original joint definitions.
